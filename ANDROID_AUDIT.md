## 1. Back Button & Navigation

### CRITICAL

**1.1 — Auth gate modal has no `onRequestClose` when mandatory**
- **File:** `components/auth/AuthSheet.tsx:96`
- **Problem:** `onRequestClose={dismissible ? handleDismiss : undefined}` — when the auth sheet is mandatory (`dismissible=false`), `onRequestClose` is `undefined`.
- **Android breakage:** Android hardware back button will either do nothing (trapping the user) or dismiss the modal, bypassing the mandatory auth gate entirely. React Native's behavior for `undefined` on Android is inconsistent across versions.
- **Severity:** CRITICAL

### HIGH

**1.2 — Full player overlay has no back button handling**
- **File:** `components/audio/FullPlayerOverlay.tsx` (entire file)
- **Problem:** The full audio player is rendered as an overlay `View`, not a `Modal`. There is zero `BackHandler` usage anywhere in the codebase (confirmed via grep). When the full player is expanded, the Android back button will navigate away from the screen entirely instead of minimizing the player.
- **Android breakage:** Users will lose their audio playback state when pressing back, expecting it to minimize/collapse the player.
- **Severity:** HIGH

**1.3 — Reading screen audio not dismissed on hardware back**
- **File:** `app/reading/[id].tsx`
- **Problem:** No `BackHandler` listener. When user presses Android back from the reading screen, the Expo Router will pop the screen, but audio continues playing in the background with no way to return to the player context for that snippet.
- **Android breakage:** Orphaned audio playback after back navigation.
- **Severity:** HIGH

**1.4 — Tab navigator has no back button guard**
- **File:** `app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx`
- **Problem:** No `BackHandler` override. On Android, pressing back on the Home tab will exit the app. Standard Android behavior is to ask "Press back again to exit" or show a confirmation, especially for apps with user state.
- **Android breakage:** Accidental app exit from Home tab.
- **Severity:** HIGH

**1.5 — Completed readings screen back navigation**
- **File:** `app/completed-readings.tsx`
- **Problem:** Uses custom `headerLeft` with manual `router.back()`, but no `BackHandler` integration. If the keyboard is open during search, back button should dismiss keyboard first, then navigate.
- **Android breakage:** Back press may navigate away while keyboard is still active instead of dismissing it first.
- **Severity:** HIGH

### MEDIUM

**1.6 — Share card modal may trap user**
- **File:** `components/share/ShareCardModal.tsx:58`
- **Problem:** Has `onRequestClose={onClose}` — correctly handled. However, if the capture-and-share flow is in progress, back press may interrupt it mid-operation.
- **Severity:** MEDIUM

**1.7 — Reading menu dropdown**
- **File:** `components/ReadingMenu.tsx:56`
- **Problem:** Has `onRequestClose={close}` — correctly handled. But it's rendered as an absolutely positioned View inside a Modal, which may have z-index issues on some Android devices.
- **Severity:** LOW

## 2. Fonts & Devanagari Text

### CRITICAL

**2.1 — `fontFamily: 'System'` has no Devanagari support on many Android devices**
- **Files:**
  - `components/SnippetContent.tsx:457` — `sanskritPreview`
  - `components/snippet/VerseBlock.tsx:76` — `sanskritText`
  - `components/audio/AudioVerseBlock.tsx:158` — `sanskritText`
- **Problem:** iOS's "System" font (SF Pro) includes Devanagari glyphs. Android's "System" font (Roboto) does NOT include Devanagari. The system falls back to Noto Sans Devanagari if available — but this is not guaranteed on all Android OEMs/versions.
- **Android breakage:** Sanskrit verses may render as tofu (□□□) or with inconsistent fallback fonts on Android devices without Noto Sans Devanagari.
- **Severity:** CRITICAL

**2.2 — No Devanagari font bundled in assets**
- **File:** `assets/fonts/` (directory does not exist)
- **Problem:** The app bundles zero custom fonts. All Devanagari rendering depends on platform system fonts. On iOS this works; on Android it's a gamble.
- **Android breakage:** Core app content (Sanskrit verses, Hindi translations) may be unreadable.
- **Severity:** CRITICAL

### HIGH

**2.3 — `fontFamily: 'Georgia'` not available on Android**
- **Files (8 occurrences):**
  - `components/TodayCard.tsx:168` — verse text
  - `components/TodayCard.tsx:242` — reflection text

  - `components/audio/AudioVerseBlock.tsx:161` — translationText
- **Problem:** Georgia is not bundled on Android. React Native silently falls back to Roboto, which changes the entire visual identity of the app. All reading content, the home card, share cards, and settings previews will look wrong.
- **Android breakage:** App loses its serif typographic identity. All content text renders in Roboto (sans-serif).
- **Severity:** HIGH

**2.4 — `letterSpacing` clips last character on Android**

  - `components/SnippetContent.tsx:466` — `letterSpacing: 1`

- **Android breakage:** Section headers ("CHAPTER 1 · VERSES 1-3") will have their last character partially cut off. Visible on all screens.

### MEDIUM

**2.5 — `lineHeight` may be insufficient for Devanagari matras**
- **Files:** `components/snippet/VerseBlock.tsx`, `components/audio/AudioVerseBlock.tsx`, `components/SnippetContent.tsx`
- **Problem:** Devanagari characters with matras (vowel marks) extend above and below the baseline more than Latin characters. Default/tight `lineHeight` values may cause clipping of top matras (e.g., ी, ै) or bottom matras (e.g., ु, ू) on Android.
- **Android breakage:** Visual clipping of Hindi/Sanskrit text at top/bottom of lines.
- **Severity:** MEDIUM

**2.6 — `fontWeight: '800'` may not render correctly**

- **Severity:** MEDIUM

## 3. Apple-Only Code & Platform Gaps

### CRITICAL

**3.1 — Apple Sign-In has no Android equivalent**
- **Files:**
  - `components/auth/AuthSheet.tsx:151` — `{Platform.OS === 'ios' && (` Apple button)
  - `components/auth/AuthGateScreen.tsx:115` — `{Platform.OS === 'ios' && (` Apple button)
  - `components/settings/ProfileSection.tsx:151` — `{Platform.OS === 'ios' && (` Apple button)
  - `app/auth/login.tsx:163` — `{Platform.OS === 'ios' && (` Apple button)
- **Problem:** Apple Sign-In is properly gated behind `Platform.OS === 'ios'` — so it won't crash. But Android users ONLY get Google Sign-In as an auth option. If Google Play Services isn't available (Huawei, Amazon Fire, some Chinese OEMs), the user has **zero auth options**.
- **Android breakage:** No auth fallback for Android devices without Google Play Services. App may be completely unusable on those devices.
- **Severity:** CRITICAL (for devices without Play Services) / HIGH (for standard Android)

### HIGH

**3.2 — Rate App opens Apple App Store on Android**
- **File:** `components/settings/SupportSection.tsx:18-21`
- **Code:**
  ```js
  const storeUrl = Platform.select({
    ios: `itms-apps://itunes.apple.com/app/id${CONFIG.APP_STORE_ID}?action=write-review`,
    default: CONFIG.APP_STORE_URL, // "https://apps.apple.com/app/10-minute-gita/id6758332047"
  });
  ```
- **Problem:** The `default` fallback uses `CONFIG.APP_STORE_URL` which points to the Apple App Store, not Google Play. `Linking.openURL` on Android will try to open this in a browser — showing the Apple App Store page (not installable) instead of Google Play.
- **Android breakage:** "Rate App" sends Android users to the wrong store. Confusing UX, zero reviews collected on Google Play.
- **Severity:** HIGH

**3.3 — `CONFIG.APP_STORE_URL` is Apple-only, no Play Store URL**
- **File:** `constants/config.ts:26`
- **Code:** `APP_STORE_URL: 'https://apps.apple.com/app/10-minute-gita/id6758332047'`
- **Problem:** No `PLAY_STORE_URL` constant exists. This URL is used as a fallback in `SupportSection.tsx` line 26.
- **Android breakage:** All store-related features send users to Apple's site.
- **Severity:** HIGH (required for Rate App fix)

**3.4 — iOS background audio mode declared, nothing for Android**
- **File:** `app.json:24-26`
- **Code:** `"UIBackgroundModes": ["audio"]` under `ios.infoPlist`
- **Problem:** iOS gets background audio via `UIBackgroundModes`. Android needs a foreground service for background audio playback. No Android foreground service is configured in `app.json` or via any Expo plugin.
- **Android breakage:** Audio will stop when the app goes to background on Android. This is the #1 expected feature for an audio/podcast app.
- **Severity:** HIGH

### MEDIUM

**3.5 — `app.json` missing `playStoreUrl`**
- **File:** `app.json` (android section, line 36-44)
- **Problem:** No `playStoreUrl` in the android config. Expo uses this for deep linking and update prompts.
- **Severity:** MEDIUM

**3.6 — Lock screen controls are iOS-only**
- **File:** `contexts/AudioPlayerContext.tsx:386-392`
- **Code:**
  ```js
  player.setActiveForLockScreen(true, {
    title: snippet.title,
    artist: '10 Minute Gita',
  }, {
    showSeekForward: true,
    showSeekBackward: true,
  });
  ```
- **Problem:** `setActiveForLockScreen` is an expo-audio API that maps to iOS's MPNowPlayingInfoCenter. On Android, lock screen / notification media controls require a foreground service with `MediaSession`. expo-audio does not automatically provide this on Android.
- **Android breakage:** No lock screen controls, no notification media controls. User cannot pause/skip without opening the app.
- **Severity:** MEDIUM (depends on background audio fix — if audio can't play in background, lock screen controls are moot)

## 4. Audio System

### HIGH

**4.1 — No audio focus handling on Android**
- **File:** `contexts/AudioPlayerContext.tsx` (entire file)
- **Problem:** No `AudioManager.requestAudioFocus()` or equivalent. On iOS, the system handles audio ducking automatically. On Android, the app must explicitly request and manage audio focus.
- **Android breakage:** Audio may play simultaneously with other apps (phone calls, navigation, music). When a phone call comes in, the Gita audio won't pause. When the call ends, it won't resume.
- **Severity:** HIGH

**4.2 — No headphone/Bluetooth disconnect handling**
- **File:** `contexts/AudioPlayerContext.tsx` (entire file)
- **Problem:** No listener for `ACTION_AUDIO_BECOMING_NOISY` (Android broadcast when headphones are unplugged or Bluetooth disconnects). iOS handles this automatically.
- **Android breakage:** Audio will switch to speaker at full volume when headphones are unplugged — embarrassing in public/quiet settings.
- **Severity:** HIGH

**4.3 — Speed control may use unsupported API**
- **File:** `contexts/AudioPlayerContext.tsx` (speed-related code)
- **Problem:** `player.rate = speed` or `player.playbackRate` — expo-audio wraps AVPlayer on iOS (reliable speed control) and Android's MediaPlayer (speed control added in API 23, potentially buggy on older devices).
- **Android breakage:** Speed changes may silently fail or cause audio glitches on Android API < 23 (Android 6.0).
- **Severity:** MEDIUM

### MEDIUM

**4.4 — 50ms polling interval for audio position**
- **File:** `hooks/useAudioPosition.ts:71`
- **Code:** `const interval = setInterval(poll, 50);`
- **Problem:** 20 polls/second. Each poll calls `player.currentTime` which crosses the JS-native bridge. On mid/low-end Android devices, this can cause UI jank, especially combined with word-by-word highlight re-renders.
- **Android breakage:** Laggy audio highlighting on budget Android devices (Redmi, Samsung A-series).
- **Severity:** MEDIUM (see also Performance section)

**4.5 — 13+ `console.log` debug statements in AudioPlayerContext**
- **File:** `contexts/AudioPlayerContext.tsx` (lines 178, 180, 216, 248, 253, 336, 425, 435, 445, 454, 460, 478, 611)
- **Problem:** These are bare `console.log` calls, not `logger.warn`/`logger.error`. They bypass the `__DEV__` guard in `logger.ts` and will execute in production builds. Each log crosses the native bridge on Android.
- **Android breakage:** Performance overhead from bridge-crossing console logs in production. Also violates codebase convention (CLAUDE.md: "never use bare console.error/warn/log").
- **Severity:** MEDIUM

## 9. Performance

### HIGH

**9.1 — 50ms polling + word-by-word re-render on budget devices**
- **Files:** `hooks/useAudioPosition.ts:71`, `components/audio/AudioVerseBlock.tsx`, `components/audio/AudioParagraph.tsx`
- **Problem:** 20 state updates/second from polling, each triggering re-render of highlight components. iOS devices have powerful GPUs that handle this smoothly. - **Files:** `assets/images/icon.png`, `assets/images/adaptive-icon.png`, `assets/images/splash-icon.png`
- **Problem:** These may be oversized (designed for iOS @3x). Android adaptive icons have specific size requirements. Oversized images increase APK size.

**9.3 — No image caching for share cards**
- **File:** `utils/shareCard.ts`
- **Problem:** Share cards are generated fresh each time using `react-native-view-shot`. On slower Android devices, capture may take longer.
- **Severity:** LOW
